# Demo script

Target duration: 2 minutes 30 seconds. Show the working app in the first 15 seconds.

1. Open the live page in a WebMCP-capable browser; point out the shared cart, capability console, and ledger.
2. Ask: “Find the cheapest blue dog harness in stock, add a medium to my cart, and buy it.”
3. Show the agent inspect capabilities, search, verify availability, and call `add_to_cart` once.
4. Show the same line appear in the human cart and the ledger record the mutation.
5. Show `prepare_checkout_review`, followed by one `create_checkout` call.
6. Highlight the `built_and_refusing`, `operator_approval_required`, and `retryable: false` fields.
7. Confirm the agent stops, reports that the cart is ready, and does not claim payment or purchase.
8. Run the three attack-proof buttons and close on the non-goals: no order execution, payment, or bypass.

Use original narration or properly licensed text-to-speech. Do not add copyrighted music or third-party marks without permission. Publish the final video publicly on YouTube and keep it under three minutes.
